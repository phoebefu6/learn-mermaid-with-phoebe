Why typed beats dragged

A diagram you typed lives in the repo next to the thing it describes. It diffs, it reviews, and it can be wrong in a pull request rather than wrong on a wall.

SEC-06 THE THREE TOOLS THAT TAME A TANGLE

Subgraphs, for structureA flat list of thirty nodes is a tangle. Grouped into four named regions it is a diagram. Ask: what are the three or four areas this really has?
Classes, for meaningColour that repeats is a legend. Colour that does not repeat is decoration. Ask: does every fill mean exactly one thing?
Hygiene, for the next readerConsistent direction, short labels, no crossing where a reorder would do. Ask: can someone follow this without me narrating?
The spaghetti testIf you cannot trace one path from entry to exit without losing your place, the diagram is not finished. Ask: where does a new event enter, and where does it end?
Session 2 is the one people thank you for

Session 1 gives you nodes and arrows, which is enough to make a mess quickly. Session 2 is the difference between a diagram you drew and a diagram someone else can read.
